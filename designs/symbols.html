Component language

Water-analogy symbols

One visual vocabulary for building, inspecting, and teaching circuits.

Pump

Voltage source

Pressure rise corresponds to electrical potential.

Restriction

Resistor

A narrow passage creates pressure loss as flow increases.

Accumulator

Capacitor

An elastic chamber stores volume and pressure.

Flywheel

Inductor

Rotating inertia resists changes in flow.

Check valve

Diode

Flow passes in one direction and blocks in reverse.

Shutoff valve

Switch

A controlled valve opens or closes the path.

Coupled wheels

Transformer

Coupled rotors exchange pressure and flow by a ratio.

Gauge

Probe / meter

A dial reads pressure or flow without changing the model.
