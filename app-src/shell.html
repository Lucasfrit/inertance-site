Editing

Pick a part in the Builder, then click the canvas to place it (R rotates, Esc cancels). Choose Connect, click to start a pipe and click again to finish. Circles along pipes create branches; nearby terminals snap. Red rings mark loose ends. Drag empty space to pan. Drag a part to move it with its pipes; Shift-drag detaches it. Drop a loose two-terminal part onto a highlighted pipe to insert it in series. Crossings do not connect.

Click a switch body to toggle it. A diode is a swing check valve that only opens with forward flow. Click a part or pipe to probe its voltage and current.

What the simulation assumes

Startup from zero stored energy. Educational switched RLC solver (backward Euler), not SPICE: DC and sine supplies, resistors, capacitors, inductors, 2–4 coupled windings (k = 1 by default), PWM switches and up to eight diodes (forward drop + 0.01 Ω, no reverse current or recovery). Wires are ideal. Leakage mode includes an inspectable primary RC snubber for recognized flyback and forward circuits. Saturation and reverse recovery are not modeled. Floating nodes and interrupted inductor current are reported rather than hidden.

Model & protection

Ripple is the default. Ideal uses a periodic ripple-neglected course reduction where validated. Other circuits use ideal components with finite L/C dynamics, preserving charging and oscillations; the model note identifies this fallback. Ideal always uses ideal conduction. Ripple retains finite L/C and startup. Leakage is incomplete and experimental: only recognized flyback and forward circuits currently support its primary RC snubber. Every active mode drives water pressure, particles, stored charge, meters and plots.
